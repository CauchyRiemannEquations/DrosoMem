The genuinely new question is unconditioned **paired historical-value tracking**
under one observation-noise setting, using frozen ZERO-carry iid ridge heads and
fresh streams. It is not autonomous recall, a structural real-versus-null gap,
an allocation superiority comparison, or a calibration sweep. Noise will be
isotropic in the frozen decoder's standardized48-coordinate input space;
raw offsets use its exact old floored scale, not a newly fitted SD or a claim
of physical per-neuron noise. No clipping/recurrence feedback is introduced.
The same draw is shared by original/replacement/reference worlds, preserving
their common-noise control. Old methods are useful implementation/provenance
references, not statistical confirmation of the new endpoint.

Before outcomes, register sigma=.10, one new noise draw per fixed anchor,
new iid/noise seeds, fixed lag2/64 anchors/six primary plus three paired secondary,
unchanged M5 scaler/head,90% joint threshold, independent replay and stopping.
The moderate synthetic dose is an operational stress condition, not a noise
level chosen from new scores or physiological calibration. A FAIL is retained
without smaller doses, extra draws/seeds, adaptation or P3.

Historical raw results outside the sparse checkout are protected by Git identity;
this is a scope/provenance audit, not a claim of rerunning all old noise paths.

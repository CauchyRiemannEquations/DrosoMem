The existing P2 generator exchanges postsynaptic endpoints within role groups
while weights remain attached to presynaptic edges. If the two raw weights
differ, postsynaptic incoming weight multisets and row normalization change.
Its structural audit explicitly counts changed incoming strengths. Thus simply
freezing its real-only readout would conflate structural comparison with head
transfer to a different representation; prior transfer negatives also warn
against treating refit and frozen transfer as the same endpoint.

M8 first asks a **construction-feasibility** question. Exchange only delayed
edges of the SAME signed raw weight and pre/post roles; lock all KC→MBON edges.
That preserves both incoming and outgoing raw weight multisets and therefore
incoming-L1 factors/current coefficients. It is more constrained than P2 and
may have substantial immobile structure. A finite generator failure does not
prove no such graph exists. No new graph or prototype outcome has yet been
used to choose seeds, budgets, overlap or numerical rank thresholds.

The separate engineered delay reference will preserve input code/state-count/
observation/head budgets while explicitly changing dynamics and degree/role/
weight/path constraints. It is a transparent generic-history positive-control
candidate, not a matched connectome null or a biological neuron-copy model.
Current and lag2 channels are both reserved before rank checks; no favorable
observation subset will be selected afterward.

Resource choice uses measured P2/4B construction and verification costs, not
performance: ten primary constructions, three whole secondary constructions,
one worker and7200s/4GiB per stage. M8 executes no source-graph trajectories,
train/test streams or fitted decoders. Performance, if design is READY, needs
a distinct later protocol with graph-specific matched fitting then frozen test
heads. No later performance result is promised or counted as complete here.

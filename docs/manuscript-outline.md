## Working title

**Historical Input Access and Limits of Rule Continuation in Fixed
Fly-Connectome Computational Reservoirs**

## Draft abstract

Fixed computational reservoirs constrained by source-derived fly connectivity
can retain decodable information about past inputs, but historical access does
not by itself establish special wiring advantages or mathematical rule learning.
We synthesize separately registered studies of iid temporal decoding, current/
history interventions, acyclic propagation, structural control feasibility,
functional sensitivity, frozen-readout interventions and affine-rule continuation.
Short-lag historical access and bounded past-value tracking satisfy their
registered criteria. An acyclic zero-carry source subset supports near-perfect
lag-2 decoding, while an exact same-node all-lag cycle-matched control is
structurally infeasible. Local response attenuation remains distinct from
decoding and structural reachability. Material real-wiring advantages fail in
the tested conditional ensembles, including a ceiling-limited tracking task.
Frozen tracking also fails one registered synthetic observation-noise condition.
An identifiable held-out affine-coefficient benchmark separates seen-rule
context lookup from supplied-form inference; its matched fixed-reservoir/affine-
head comparison fails both acquisition and autonomous continuation requirements.
We preserve all negative, undefined and feasibility outcomes and their independent
checks. The evidence supports operational historical-input access within these
model interfaces, while limiting claims about fine wiring, feedback-cycle
specificity, biological learning and rule transfer.

## 5. Discussion: narrow claims, negative results and open questions

Historical traces are accessible through selected fixed model/readout interfaces.
That does not identify anatomical storage, special real wiring, biological
synaptic learning or a mathematical generalization mechanism. M3/M4 constrain
cycle attribution in different ways; M5 constrains response magnitude; M7
constrains frozen-readout robustness; M9 is ceiling-limited; M11 is acquisition-
limited. Treat these distinct explanations separately.

Prospective future learner/rule-family/physiological studies can test new
questions, but are not completion steps or repairs to the current failures.
P3 is excluded. Avoid a conclusion that all fine wiring is irrelevant, all
reservoirs cannot learn rules or computational seeds represent animals.
